### 🛠️ What I’m building

I’m currently building **StreamPatch**. I also built [**SponsorSkip**](https://github.com/anuvikdas/YTSponsorSkip), a Chrome extension that detects and skips sponsor reads on YouTube.

> **Where would you end the skip?** A creator plugs their own photography presets, goes back to teaching, then reads a paid Squarespace ad. SponsorSkip grouped all three into one skip.

<details>
<summary><strong>▶ See what I found</strong></summary>

The preset plug and the paid read were two separate segments. Grouping them meant viewers would lose part of the photography lesson in between.

That’s why I review videos by hand and measure two things separately: how much of the sponsor read the extension missed and how much regular footage it would have skipped.

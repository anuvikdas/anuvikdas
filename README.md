## What I’m building

I’m building **StreamPatch** right now. A project I’ve been working on is [**SponsorSkip**](https://github.com/anuvikdas/YTSponsorSkip), a Chrome extension that detects and skips sponsor reads on YouTube.

### Where would you end the skip?

A creator plugs their own photography presets, goes back to teaching, and later reads a paid Squarespace ad. SponsorSkip grouped all three into one skip. Where would you split it?

<details>
<summary>See what I found</summary>

The preset plug and the Squarespace ad were two separate promotions, with actual photography instruction in between. If the extension treated them as one segment, viewers would lose part of the tutorial.

That case shows why I review videos by hand and track two things separately: how much of the sponsor read the extension missed and how much regular footage it would have skipped.

</details>
